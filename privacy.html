Privacy Policy

Last updated September 8, 2026

FixMyBlockFence.com is operated as a specialized block-wall repair service of Desert Dreamco LLC.

Information you may submit

When you request an estimate or evaluation, you may provide information such as your name, phone number, email address, property ZIP code, property type, project details, approximate wall measurements, and photographs of the wall or property.

How we use the information

The information you submit is used to review your repair request, understand the visible wall condition, contact you about the project, determine appropriate next steps, and prepare or coordinate an estimate or on-site evaluation when applicable.

Photographs and project information

Please upload only photographs and information relevant to the repair request. Avoid uploading identification documents, financial information, medical information, or other unrelated sensitive material.

Form provider

The estimate form is hosted by Tally. Information submitted through that form may also be processed by Tally according to Tally's own privacy, security, and data-processing practices.

Website analytics and advertising measurement

This website may use analytics or advertising measurement tools to understand website traffic, calls-to-action, and lead activity. These tools may process technical information such as browser, device, page-view, or referral information according to their own policies when they are enabled.

How information may be handled

Information may be accessed by Desert Dreamco LLC personnel and service providers that support the website, form processing, communications, analytics, or advertising functions needed to operate the service. Information should be used only as reasonably necessary for those purposes.

Data retention

Project inquiries and related information may be retained for as long as reasonably needed to respond to the request, maintain project or business records, address follow-up questions, or meet applicable business and legal requirements.
