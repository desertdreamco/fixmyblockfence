Block Wall Pilaster & Column Repair

Pilasters and masonry columns often define wall panels, support gates, or reinforce long wall runs. Cracking or displacement at a pilaster can affect the adjoining wall and should be scoped as more than a cosmetic crack when movement is visible.

What we look for

Visible conditions that can change the repair scope.

  • Cracked masonry pilaster
  • Displaced or leaning column
  • Damage at a gate hinge or gate opening
  • Separated wall-to-pilaster connection
  • Impact-damaged column
  • Partial pilaster reconstruction

Our process

How we determine the right repair scope.

1

Photograph the full column and adjoining wall panels

2

Include gate hardware and opening if the pilaster is gate-related

3

Determine whether damage is superficial, localized or structural

4

Remove and rebuild failed masonry when required

5

Restore the wall line and gate-adjacent area within the approved scope

Questions

Common questions about block wall pilaster & column repair.

Can a single pilaster be rebuilt?

Yes, when the surrounding conditions allow a localized reconstruction.

Do you repair gate-related wall damage?

Yes. We can repair the masonry portion around damaged gate openings and pilasters.

Will a cracked pilaster always need demolition?

No. The correct scope depends on the extent and type of damage.
