What we look for

Visible conditions that can change the repair scope.

  • Visible lean or bow
  • Stair-step or horizontal cracks near the leaning section
  • Separated pilasters or gate columns
  • Movement at the base or footing
  • Erosion, irrigation, root or drainage-related conditions
  • Loose or displaced block and mortar

Questions

Common questions about leaning block wall repair in phoenix & the valley.

Can a leaning block wall be repaired without replacing the entire wall?

Sometimes. If movement is localized and the surrounding wall remains suitable, a partial reconstruction may be possible. A severely unstable or broadly displaced wall may require a larger rebuild.

What should I photograph?

Send a straight-on photo, a side-angle that shows the lean, the wall base, nearby pilasters and any visible cracks.

Should I push on the wall to see if it moves?

No. If the wall appears unstable, avoid applying pressure to it. Photos and an on-site evaluation are safer ways to assess the condition.
