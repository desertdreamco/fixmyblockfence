Water, Irrigation & Erosion Damage Around Block Walls

Arizona block walls can be affected by repeated irrigation, monsoon runoff, erosion around the base, moisture migration and soil movement. Repairing the masonry without recognizing the surrounding condition can lead to repeat damage, so we document visible water and erosion clues during the repair assessment.

What we look for

Visible conditions that can change the repair scope.

  • Erosion or soil loss near the wall base
  • Irrigation repeatedly wetting the wall or footing area
  • Efflorescence or moisture staining
  • Cracking associated with settlement or washout
  • Loose block or mortar in persistently wet areas
  • Damage after heavy monsoon runoff

Questions

Common questions about water, irrigation & erosion damage around block walls.

Does efflorescence mean the wall is structurally damaged?

Not necessarily. It indicates moisture movement through masonry, but structural significance depends on the broader wall condition.

Can irrigation contribute to wall problems?

Repeated saturation or erosion near the base can be relevant, especially when combined with settlement or visible movement.

Do you repair drainage systems?

Fix My Block Fence is focused on the wall repair. Related site or irrigation work can be discussed separately through Desert Dreamco when it falls within the licensed scope.
