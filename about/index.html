Meet Roberto Juarez

Hands-on project leadership.

Roberto Juarez leads Desert Dreamco projects with hands-on involvement in project evaluation, estimating, construction operations and quality control. His background includes construction management, project management and property-damage evaluation.

That experience helps the company look beyond a visible crack or damaged block. The surrounding wall, pilasters, alignment, base conditions and site conditions all matter when deciding whether the appropriate scope is a localized repair, partial reconstruction or a larger rebuild.

When engineering or work outside the company’s licensed scope may be required, that limitation is identified rather than replaced with an unsupported recommendation.

How we approach repairs

Repair the damage without overselling the project.

Not every damaged block wall needs complete replacement. The appropriate solution depends on the actual condition found and how the affected masonry connects to the surrounding wall.

Evaluate the visible condition

We review cracking, movement, alignment, loose masonry, nearby pilasters and visible base conditions before recommending a scope.

Match the scope to the damage

The work may involve a localized repair, partial demolition and reconstruction, pilaster rebuilding or replacement of a larger section.
