Block wall, block fence, CMU wall

Block wall, block fence, cinder block or CMU wall?

Homeowners and property managers use different names for concrete masonry perimeter walls. We repair the wall based on its actual condition—whether you call it a block wall, block fence, cinder block wall or CMU wall.

Block Wall Repair

General CMU wall repair and reconstruction.

Block Fence Repair

Perimeter and privacy block-fence damage.

Cinder Block Wall Repair

Common homeowner wording for concrete masonry walls.

CMU / Masonry Wall Repair

Contractor and commercial terminology for concrete masonry units.

Repair vs. rebuild

Not every damaged wall needs complete replacement.

We scope the affected section based on the visible condition of the wall, nearby pilasters, alignment, base conditions and the surrounding masonry. Depending on what we find, the right scope may be localized repair, partial demolition and reconstruction, pilaster rebuilding, or a larger replacement.

  • Localized cracked-block or mortar repair when appropriate
  • Partial wall reconstruction
  • Pilaster or column reconstruction
  • Vehicle-impact damage repair
  • Collapsed-section rebuilding
  • Footing-related reconstruction when the wall condition requires it

Something looks unsafe?

If a wall is visibly leaning, loose, partially collapsed or moving, keep people away from it and do not test it by pushing on the wall. Send photos from a safe position and we can help determine the next step.
